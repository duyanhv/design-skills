Color carries meaning, but never alone.

## Rules

### Contrast
- **MUST** Ensure text meets a contrast ratio of 4.5:1 against its background. — `4.5:1` [src](https://example.invalid/design/lumen/color#Contrast) `lumen-ds/color/001`
  - Why: Large text may use 3:1.
  - The swatches below show the accessible pairings: _[figure: A grid of foreground and background color pairings with their contrast ratios.]_
- **MUST** Avoid using color as the only way to convey information. [src](https://example.invalid/design/lumen/color#Contrast) `lumen-ds/color/002`
  - Why: Pair color with a label, an icon, or a pattern.
